Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

This page explains what information Sentinel Tech - QA collects through this website, how it is used, and how you can request access to or deletion of your data. We keep this deliberately short and accurate.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through the contact form: your name, work email, and the message you write. We do not ask for any other personal data.

How the contact form works

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, which delivers your message to our team by email. We use that information solely to respond to your inquiry. We never sell or rent it, and we do not share it beyond what is needed to reply to you.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (Vercel Web Analytics and Cloudflare) to understand traffic patterns — for example, which pages are viewed. These tools do not build advertising profiles of you, and the site does not set marketing or tracking cookies.

Data retention and your rights

We retain contact messages only as long as needed to follow up. You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time by emailing contato@sentinelqa.tech.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us at contato@sentinelqa.tech.
